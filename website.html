The story

Start with the problem.

I contributed to the redevelopment of Sitemax’s website by shaping the structure and customer journey, providing wireframes and creative assets, writing website copy and collaborating with external developer Bear Marketing.

The opportunity

The website needed to represent a broad technical product range while making it easier for customers to understand the offering and find relevant information.

My contribution

Created the information structure and wireframes; wrote customer-focused copy; prepared creative assets; worked with the development agency through delivery.

Outcome & perspective

GA4 reported 334.7% more active users and 338.3% more new users in 2025–26 compared with 2022–23. These figures show overall website growth, not an isolated measure of the redevelopment’s impact.

Reporting basis: Google Analytics 4, 1 Jul 2025 – 30 Jun 2026 versus 1 Jul 2022 – 30 Jun 2023. The figures are for the website as a whole.
