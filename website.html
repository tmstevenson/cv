The opportunity

Make the complex feel simple.

Sitemax supplies technical scaffold containment, construction signage and site services. Its website needed to communicate that breadth without making customers work hard to understand what was available or where to start.

The opportunity was to create a clearer path through the offering, with information that speaks to the needs of the people specifying, purchasing and using these solutions.

My role

Shaping the experience, not writing the code.

I worked with external developers to plan and deliver the replacement website. I led the customer journey, site structure, wireframes, copywriting and creative direction, alongside implementing SEO, Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) and website security measures. Improving search visibility, making content easier for search engines and AI-powered answer tools to understand, and reducing spam were important parts of the strategy.

Structure & user journeysPlanned how customers would discover products, services and useful information.
Wireframes & contentMapped pages, developed the content approach and wrote the website copy.
SEO & AEOImplemented search and answer-engine optimisation across site content and structure to improve discoverability and support organic user growth.
Website securityImplemented security and anti-spam measures to reduce unwanted enquiries and protect the quality of incoming leads.
Creative & collaborationPrepared visual assets, briefed the developers and worked through the build with external developers.
What matters

A better way to find, understand and enquire.

Good website work isn't just a redesign. SEO and AEO made discoverability a priority, while security and anti-spam improvements reduced unwanted submissions. Alongside a clearer experience, the live website brings Sitemax's products, services and customer needs together in one place.
