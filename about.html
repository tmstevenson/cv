About me

Driven by outcomes.
Powered by people.

I believe marketing works best when commercial thinking, creativity and genuine collaboration come together.

How I work

Ambitious about the work. Invested in the people.

I'm a marketing leader who enjoys seeing the bigger picture, making complex ideas easier to understand and turning opportunities into tangible outcomes. I bring structure and momentum to projects without losing sight of the people they're designed for.

I value clarity, initiative and follow-through. Whether I'm building a strategy, improving a customer journey or working across departments, I want everyone to understand the goal and feel part of making it happen.

A little more insight

Two perspectives on how I lead.

My personality assessments describe patterns that resonate with my approach to work. They're reflective tools, not qualifications or substitutes for demonstrated results.

Strategic. Adaptable. Outcome-focused.

The Achiever profile emphasises ambition, strategic thinking, adaptability and commitment to delivering on goals. For me, the most useful takeaway is the balance between setting a high standard and keeping the work purposeful.
