The story

Start with the problem.

I identified the opportunity for a customer-specific B2B signage purchasing portal that feels familiar to business buyers and makes reordering simpler.

The opportunity

Complex or repetitive purchasing can create unnecessary friction for business customers and cost suppliers repeat orders.

My contribution

Developed the concept, customer journeys, commercial objectives and requirements, then briefed external developers to build the solution.

Outcome & perspective

The intended outcomes are easier ordering, stronger customer retention and more repeat business. The portal is in development, so results are not yet available.
