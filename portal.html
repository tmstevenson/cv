The opportunity

Make it easier to buy again.

For enterprise construction clients, ordering signage and site branding can involve repeated enquiries, approvals and back-and-forth communication. I saw an opportunity to make purchasing more convenient for existing customers and give them more reasons to keep ordering through Sitemax.

The concept

A secure ordering experience tailored to each client's organisation, giving their teams access to approved products, agreed pricing and previous portal orders. The aim is to make routine purchasing simpler while maintaining consistent branding across projects.

My contribution

I initiated the idea and worked with my team to define the customer experience, commercial objectives, platform scope and functional requirements. I also created a visual mockup to communicate the concept before handing the specification to external developers.

What the portal is designed to achieve

  • Make purchasing and repeat ordering easier for project teams.
  • Give customers a familiar, company-branded ordering experience.
  • Reduce unnecessary administration and ordering errors.
  • Strengthen customer experience, retention and repeat business.
Work in progress

The concept, scope and mockup have been developed. The platform has not launched, so customer adoption and commercial outcomes are not yet available.
